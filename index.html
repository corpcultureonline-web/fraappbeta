What is a Franchise Readiness Audit?

What is a Franchise Readiness Audit?

A structured assessment of whether a business can be franchised successfully. It measures seven areas and includes a separate legal and trademark check.

How is my score calculated?

Seven areas are weighted by how much they determine franchise success, including whether the numbers work and the operating system can be repeated.

Does it cost anything?

No. The audit is free, takes around four minutes, and does not require an account or signup.

Is the audit legal advice?

No. The legal check is an initial snapshot, not legal advice or a substitute for professional counsel.
