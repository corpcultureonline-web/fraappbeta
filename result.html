What we noticed

1. Your training programme is the piece still in progress.

A franchise owner leans on training hardest in their first 90 days. Yours is partly built, which is a good start — and completing it is largely writing down what you already do.

2. You are not the bottleneck you could have been.

A manager already runs the day to day within limits you set. The next step is writing those limits down, so a franchise owner’s manager can work to the same rules.

3. Every sale so far comes from one city.

What works in Chennai, where people know you, has to be shown to work where they do not. An investor elsewhere is taking a risk you have not yet removed.

4. Your brand is not yet pulling partners towards you.

Only 1 to 5 people asked about a franchise last year. You will need to go out and find partners, rather than wait for them.
